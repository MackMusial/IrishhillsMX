    A private track in Irish Hills MI.

    IrishHillsMX is a private motocross track on Sharon Hollow Road in Manchester, Michigan. It isn't open to the public: ride as a member, or come out as a guest rider.

    Next to the main track there's a separate kids track, so the whole family has somewhere to ride, from first laps to years of racing. Track conditions, events and any changes are posted in the club's Facebook group.

          Know before you go

          Location

          Get directions (opens Google Maps in a new tab)

          Directions go to the Irish Hills area until the exact address is added.

          Riding hours

          IrishHillsMX is a private track, not open to the public. Check the Facebook group for track conditions and changes before you head out.

          Required at the gate

          1. Cash only. Bring cash for your fee.
          2. Proof of medical insurance. It's required to ride on the track. Show it at the gate.
          3. Riders under 18 must have a parent or legal guardian with them, or a notarized letter of permission from a parent.

          Track rules

          These are common motocross safety rules. The owners still need to confirm the final list. Placeholder

          1. DOT or ECE approved helmet, goggles, boots, gloves and long sleeves and pants on the track at all times.
          2. Ride in the direction of the track only. Never stop on a blind landing or in a corner.
          3. Walking speed in the pits and parking areas.
          4. Youth riders on youth-sized bikes, supervised by an adult.
          5. No alcohol until bikes are loaded for the day.
          6. Pack out your trash and leave the pits clean.

          What to bring

          • Proof of medical insurance
          • Cash for the gate (cash only)
          • For riders under 18: a parent or legal guardian, or a notarized letter from a parent
          • Helmet, goggles, boots, gloves and riding gear
          • Chest protector and knee braces (recommended)
          • Fuel, a gas can and basic tools
          • A stand, tie-downs and a tarp for the pit
          • Plenty of water, food and snacks
          • Chairs, a canopy and sunscreen
