# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS with no build step, no backend and no database. The owners edit one clearly labeled data file for ride dates and prices.

## Users

- Members and guest riders of all ages checking when the next ride day is, what it costs and what they need at the gate.
- Parents bringing kids to ride: they need fees, gate requirements, rules, what to bring and how to get there, usually on a phone.
- The track owners, who are not developers and update dates and prices a few times a year.

## Product Purpose

IrishHillsMX is a private motocross track (not open to the public) with a separate kids track, at 14758 Sharon Hollow Rd, Manchester, MI 48158. The site tells people when the ride days are, what they cost (members and guest riders), what's required at the gate, what to expect and bring, and how to get there and stay in touch. Success: a visitor finds the next ride date and price within seconds, and the owners can update them without touching the page layout.

## Positioning

A private club track, not a public facility. Ride days are events, so the calendar is the core of the site. The club's Facebook group ("Irish Hills MX (OFFICIAL CLUB PAGE)", about 2,400 members) is the main channel for news and questions; the site points there.

## Operating Context

- Located at 14758 Sharon Hollow Rd, Manchester, MI 48158, in the Irish Hills region of southeast Michigan. (Road type "Rd" and ZIP inferred from the street's other addresses; confirm with owners.)
- Owner hosts the site on their own hardware.
- Visitors often check on phones, sometimes outdoors or on the way to the track.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fast, responsive, accessible, keyboard-friendly; respects reduced motion.
- Ride dates, fees and contact links live in one data file (`js/site-data.js`).
- Unknown details are labeled placeholders, not invented: ride dates, track description, rules specifics.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: written exactly **IrishHillsMX**, everywhere.
- The brief asks for a feel that is exciting and outdoorsy, with motocross energy balanced by clear practical info for families. Palette inspired by dirt, forest and racing.

## Evidence on Hand

- Confirmed (from owner, 2026-11-25): address; fees (members $90/year plus $15 each ride; adult guest $40 flat; kids track fee below); cash only; proof of medical insurance required and shown at the gate; riders under 18 need a parent or legal guardian present or a notarized letter from a parent; separate kids track; Facebook page and group links.
- Also confirmed: kids track $15 for non-members, free for members; riding 11:00 AM–6:00 PM; no spectator fee; merch is coming soon (no products yet).
- Still unconfirmed: ride dates, track description, general track rules, phone, email. No logo, no track photos, no testimonials. Do not fabricate any of these.
- Photos: owners will supply later. Until then, verified free stock motocross imagery may be used, labeled as stock, with marked slots for real track photos.

## Product Principles

1. The next ride date is the hero fact.
2. Practical before promotional: fees, gate requirements, rules, what to bring, directions.
3. Owners must be able to update content without a developer.
4. Never state a fact the owners haven't confirmed.
