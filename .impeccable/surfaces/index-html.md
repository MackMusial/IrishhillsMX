# IrishhillsMX home page

Scope: single-page public site (index.html). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: riders and parents checking the next public ride, price, rules and directions, mostly on phones. Owners update dates and prices in js/site-data.js.
Action: find the next ride date (and price) and see all five dates.
Constraints: no invented dates, prices, address or contacts; sample values are labeled as placeholders on the page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The season is one two-ink screenprinted race poster: five dates stacked huge, the next one circled. Refuses the dark-photo-plus-orange-button moto template and the card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Uncoated poster-paper ground, ink black, mud-orange and forest-green as the only two spot inks; photos printed as halftone duotones in one ink; slight misregistration between inks; Big Shoulders Display for stacked condensed display, Barlow for text. Sections alternate paper and full-field ink pulls.
STORY: Visitor sees the name, the next ride date and price at once, scans the five-date run, then learns what to expect, prices, rules and how to get there.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: stacked IRISH HILLS MX title, one welcome line, next-ride block (date huge, ride n of 5, adult/kid price) with orange "See all ride dates" button. Right ~55%: orange halftone roost photo bleeding off the edge. Bottom band: the five dates in a row, next one circled.
FORM: Two-Ink Race Poster, my list position 1 (pick, chosen over assigned 5). seed:1207d49ffc4f86ff
Signature motion: on load the orange ink pulls into registration and the circle draws around the next date; reduced motion shows the final print.
